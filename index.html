DrawNigh

“Draw nigh to God, and he will draw nigh to you.” — James 4:8

DrawNigh is a free Bible reading app, on the web and as a Firefox add-on.

Reading

  • King James Version, offline and free
  • New Living Translation, on the web version for now
  • Reading aloud in your device’s own voice, with speed and voice choice, working offline
  • Translators’ margin notes, off unless you want them
  • Verse of the Day, and random verses

Plans

  • Plans by finish date or by your own pace, with no deadlines
  • A gentle plan ladder that eases the target when you fall behind, never to zero
  • Catch me up
  • Digging the Well: stay in one book and go deep

Keeping track

  • Daily streak
  • Badges and ranks
  • Milestone history
  • Star verses, and Gems of Light for the verses that stop you
  • Seasons

Prayer and privacy

  • Prayer timer with gentle chimes
  • The Secret Place: a hidden room for writing to God

Moving your reading

  • Backup code, with Merge or Replace
  • QR square, or a saved file

Firefox add-on only

  • A verse card that meets you while you browse

Your reading stays yours

Everything is kept on your own device. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

Sync, when it comes, will be optional. It uses only a hidden folder in your own Google Drive, never ours.
