DrawNigh Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

DrawNigh is published by the Incorporated Trustees of Counsel of God International Outreach (CAC/IT/NO 141069), Abuja, Nigeria.

In short: DrawNigh keeps your reading on your own device. We run no server and collect nothing about you. If you turn on cloud backup, your information goes only to your own Google Drive, never to us.

What stays on your device

Your reading progress, streaks, plans, saved verses (Gems), settings and letters in The Secret Place are kept only in your browser's storage on your own device. We cannot see them.

If you clear your browser data or remove the app, this information is deleted. To keep it, make a backup code first.

Backup codes and QR squares

Backup codes and QR squares are created on your device. They go nowhere unless you copy, send or show them yourself.

Cloud backup with Google Drive (optional)

Cloud backup is off unless you turn it on. When you do, you sign in with your Google account and DrawNigh asks for one permission only: to keep its own backup file in a hidden app folder in your Google Drive. DrawNigh cannot see or open any of your other Drive files.

  • What is saved: the same reading information listed above, so another device can pick up where you stopped.
  • The Secret Place: letters are locked with your word before they leave your device, so the copy in your Drive cannot be read without it.
  • Where it goes: only to your own Google Drive. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and we never see it.
  • Turning it off: DrawNigh offers to delete the Drive copy. You can also remove DrawNigh's access at any time from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

DrawNigh's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Reading the New Living Translation (NLT)

The King James Version is built into DrawNigh and needs no connection.

If you choose to read the NLT, the app asks Tyndale House's NLT service (api.nlt.to) for the chapter you are reading. Like visiting any website, this shows Tyndale your device's internet address and which passage was asked for. No name, account or personal details are sent. Tyndale's own privacy policy covers their side.

Where the app comes from

  • Website version: served from GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep ordinary visit records, such as internet addresses. GitHub's privacy policy applies.
  • Firefox add-on: installed from Mozilla Add-ons. Mozilla counts how many people use the add-on each day without telling us who they are.

Suggestions

The suggestion form opens Google Forms, and only when you tap it. Whatever you type there reaches us through Google. Please don't put anything private in it.

Giving

DrawNigh is free. If you choose to give through the giving link, your gift goes to Counsel of God International Outreach. The payment is handled by Paystack, and your card details go to Paystack only. We never see them. Paystack's own privacy policy covers their side.

What we never do

  • No adverts
  • No tracking or analytics
  • No selling or sharing of your information

Children

DrawNigh is suitable for all ages. We collect no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
